Applying the playbook to new work

  • Frame the work - Ask what the problem is, who is affected, what evidence exists, what the business value is, what happens if we do nothing, what phase the work is in, which team owns it and whether it is in Jira.
  • Decide whether pre-discovery is needed - Use pre-discovery if the problem is unclear, strategic, cross-cutting, ownership is unclear, multiple functions are affected, the solution is being assumed too early or Design and Innovation is exploring an opportunity.
  • Understand the context - Review existing research, product pages, decision logs, MI, feedback, accessibility issues, technical constraints, operational processes and stakeholder context.
  • Plan discovery or design - Agree research questions, design questions, technical questions, participants, artefacts, success measures, release constraints and implementation constraints.
  • Create evidence-led stories - Create or update Jira with epics, stories, acceptance criteria, linked evidence, assigned team, priority, dependencies and target dates where needed.
  • Build and test - Keep design, content and BA involved, test early, test accessibility, validate with users where needed, prepare release evidence and update documentation.
  • Release and learn - Complete post-deployment checks, monitor live behaviour, collect feedback, review success measures, add improvements to backlog, update debt logs and share learning.
